## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-04

### Added

- `titleCase(stringToTransform, options)`, applying the headline-style rules of _The Chicago Manual of Style_ 8.159 and 8.161.
- `never`, `always`, and `special` options, matched case-insensitively.
- Built-in acronym, initialism, and brand capitalizations, restored from any input case and including possessives and plurals.
- ES module, CommonJS, and TypeScript declaration builds.
